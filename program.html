How to Choose Your Ideal Program

Finding the right workout program is crucial for achieving your fitness goals. Follow our guide to make the best choice.

1

Assess Your Fitness Level

Be honest about your current fitness capabilities. Choose a program that challenges you but is still manageable for your experience level.

2

Define Your Goals

Clarify what you want to achieve—weight loss, muscle gain, better endurance, or improved wellness—and select a program aligned with those goals.

3

Consider Your Schedule

Choose a program with a time commitment you can realistically maintain throughout its duration.

4

Consult with a Trainer

Still unsure? Our club members can help assess your needs and recommend the best program for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our workout programs.

Do I need previous experience to start a program?

Not at all! We offer programs for all fitness levels, including complete beginners. Our Fitness Foundations program is specifically designed for those new to exercise.

How long until I see results from a program?

Results vary based on the program, your consistency, and individual factors. Most participants notice improvements in energy and strength within 2-3 weeks, with visible physical changes typically appearing around 4-6 weeks.

Do the programs include nutritional guidance?

Yes, most of our programs include basic nutritional recommendations to support your fitness goals. You can also check out the pre-made meal plans o our website.

What equipment do I need for the programs?

Each program has different equipment requirements. Some can be done with minimal home equipment, while others utilize our full gym facilities. The program details specify what's needed before you decide.

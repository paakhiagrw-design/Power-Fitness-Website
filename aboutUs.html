About Us

Power Fitness was built by three motivated individuals who believe in fitness as a lifestyle, not a chore.

Victoria Mayowa Adeosun

I’m Victoria Mayowa Adeosun, a student born in Spain with Nigerian roots. I’m passionate about coding and constantly working to improve my programming skills, especially in finding ways technology can solve real-world problems. In addition to my studies, I enjoy staying active through fitness and believe that maintaining a balance between academic work and personal well-being is crucial for success. I’m always looking for new opportunities to learn, grow, and challenge myself, both academically and personally.

Deliwe Mufute

Hi, I’m Deliwe Tinotenda Mufute, a computer science student at Griffith College with a passion for technology, learning, and personal growth. Originally from Zimbabwe, I bring a global perspective and a curious mindset to everything I do. Outside of my academic pursuits, I enjoy playing basketball and exploring new places, as I believe that staying active and open to new experiences is key to both personal and professional development. With a strong interest in innovation and problem-solving, I’m eager to make a meaningful impact in the tech world and beyond.

Paakhi Agrawala

My name is Paakhi Agrawala, and I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (Honors) at Griffith College. Originally from India and raised in Dubai, I have always been passionate about leading an active and balanced lifestyle. Alongside my academic interests in technology and computing, I enjoy working out, swimming, and dancing as part of my daily routine. Over the years, I have also trained in sports such as badminton, volleyball, tennis, and karate. Through this platform, I aim to share my journey and inspire others to embrace fitness as a key part of their lives.

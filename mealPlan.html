Meal Planning Tips

Expert guidance to help you plan and prepare healthy meals with ease

Plan Weekly

Take time each weekend to plan your meals for the week ahead. This reduces decision fatigue and helps you stay on track with your nutrition goals.

Shop Smart

Create a shopping list based on your meal plan and stick to it. Shop the perimeter of the grocery store first where fresh foods are usually located.

Batch Cook

Dedicate a few hours to preparing meals in advance. Store them in portioned containers for easy grab-and-go options during busy days.

Portion Control

Use measuring cups and a food scale until you can eyeball portions accurately. This helps prevent accidental overeating.

Stay Hydrated

Drink water before meals to help with portion control and throughout the day to support metabolism and overall health.

80/20 Rule

Aim for nutritious choices 80% of the time while allowing 20% flexibility for treats and special occasions to maintain a sustainable approach.
